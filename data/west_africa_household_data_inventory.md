# CROSS-COUNTRY / REGIONAL DATA SOURCES

## EHCVM (Enquete Harmonisee sur les Conditions de Vie des Menages)
A harmonized household living conditions survey across WAEMU (UEMOA) member states, implemented with World Bank and WAEMU Commission support. Covers Mali, Niger, and Senegal (but NOT Ghana, which is not a WAEMU member). Two rounds: 2018-2019 and 2021-2022. Publicly available via World Bank Microdata Catalog.

## Cadre Harmonise (CH)
Biannual food security classification analyses conducted across 17 countries in West and Central Africa, including all 5 target countries. Data available at: https://data.humdata.org/dataset/cadre-harmonise

## Feed the Future
All 5 countries are Feed the Future target countries. Phase 1 surveys (Baseline ~2012-13, Interim ~2015, Endline ~2019) and Phase 2 surveys (Baseline ~2019-2020, Midline ~2021-2023) were conducted. Central repository: https://data.usaid.gov/

## LSMS-ISA
World Bank program covering Nigeria, Mali, and Niger with multi-topic, nationally representative panel household surveys with strong agriculture focus. Central catalog: https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/lsms/initiatives/lsms-ISA
