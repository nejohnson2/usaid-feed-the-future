## Notes

1. **DHS data access**: All DHS datasets require free registration at dhsprogram.com. Approval is typically granted within 1-2 business days for legitimate research purposes.

2. **LSMS-ISA data**: Among these five countries, only Ethiopia, Uganda, and Tanzania are LSMS-ISA partner countries. Kenya and Rwanda do not have LSMS surveys.

3. **MICS coverage**: Kenya is the only country with significant MICS activity, though all Kenya MICS rounds have been subnational (specific regions/counties) except the 2000 round. The other four countries rely primarily on DHS for comparable health/nutrition indicators.

4. **Rwanda CFSVA**: Rwanda stands out for having the most regular food security/nutrition assessment cycle (every 2-3 years since 2006), combining food security and nutrition data in a single survey.

5. **IPC analyses**: All five countries participate in the IPC system, with analyses conducted multiple times per year. IPC provides area-level classifications rather than household-level microdata.

6. **Feed the Future**: All five countries have been Feed the Future focus countries. Phase 1 surveys (baseline ~2012-13, interim ~2015, endline ~2018) and Phase 2 surveys (baseline ~2019-20, midline ~2021-23) have been conducted with varying timelines.

7. **Agricultural surveys**: Rwanda and Ethiopia have the most systematic annual agricultural survey programs. Kenya had a major gap in agricultural data collection before the 2024-25 census pilot.

8. **COVID-19 phone surveys**: Several countries (Ethiopia, Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania) conducted high-frequency phone surveys during 2020-2022 as part of World Bank-supported COVID-19 monitoring, which include food security modules.
